# merryxmascc.github.io

**Resume formatter:** https://merryxmascc.github.io/

A single-file, offline resume builder, based on [gracexygu/resume-formatter](https://github.com/gracexygu/resume-formatter) (MIT), with a UK-style "Word" template (Times New Roman 10pt, US Letter), English section headings, and **✎ Update section** for pasting plain text or Markdown into one section or the whole CV.

- Everything runs in the browser; nothing is uploaded.
- The page opens with a fictional sample CV. Load your own with **导入 → 导入 Markdown** or paste your CV via **✎ Update section → Whole resume**.
- `site/index.html` is the whole app. It can also be downloaded and used offline.

The previous Christmas-tree site is kept on the [`archive`](https://github.com/merryxmascc/merryxmascc.github.io/tree/archive) branch.
